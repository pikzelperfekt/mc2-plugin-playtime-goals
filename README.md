# Playtime Goals

Set a daily playtime goal, or a daily limit, and see how today is going right on
MC²'s Play screen.

- **A card on the Play screen** with today's minutes, a ring that fills toward
  your goal, your streak, and the last seven days.
- **Goal or limit.** "Play at least" counts the days you hit your goal.
  "Play at most" counts the days you stayed under it, and tells you when
  today's time is up.
- **Counts real time.** Two packs open at once count once, and a session that
  runs past midnight is split between the two days.
- **A heads-up** when you reach the goal (or the limit), once a day. You can turn
  it off.

## Install

In MC², open **Plugins › Browse**, find Playtime Goals and press **Install**.
Or use **Install from GitHub** with `pikzelperfekt/mc2-plugin-playtime-goals`.

## Settings

**Plugins › Playtime Goals › Settings**

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Goal | "Play at least" or "Play at most" |
| Minutes a day | The goal or limit, 5 to 720 |
| Tell me when I reach it | A notification once a day |

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Save plugin data | Remembers your play sessions on this computer, for the streak |
| Navigate the app | Adds the card to the Play screen |

Nothing leaves your computer. Needs MC² with plugin API 2 or newer.
